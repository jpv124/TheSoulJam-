About Us

World-Class Musicians. Unforgettable Nights.

We are the best wedding & event band in Hudson Valley. We throw high-energy dance parties and provide the finest live music for weddings, corporate events & more.

We create magical live music experiences that will keep your guests on the dance floor all night long. No lip-synching, no backing tracks, no gimmicks — just world-class musicians throwing a personalized dance party.
